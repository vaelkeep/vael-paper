Clear through the morning with high cloud arriving from the southwest after
noon. Winds light and variable, becoming southerly by evening. No measurable
rain expected before Thursday.

**Today** — High 84°, low 61°. Humidity 54 per cent. Sunrise 6:52, sunset 7:46.

**Wednesday** — High 86°, low 64°. Increasing cloud, a chance of an evening
thunderstorm inland.

**Thursday** — High 79°, low 58°. Front passes overnight; markedly cooler and
drier behind it.

The extended outlook holds the cooler air in place through the weekend, which
would make it the first genuinely autumnal stretch of the season.
