**Sir** — Your column on maintenance windows assumes an operator who is awake.
Some of us have automated ourselves into a position where the only thing that
still requires a human is the thing that goes wrong at four in the morning. This
is not obviously progress.

**Sir** — I read with interest the piece on notebook rot. I have kept the same
lab notebook, in the same format, for eleven years, and can report that the
format was never the problem. The problem was always that I stopped writing in
it during precisely the weeks that turned out to matter.
