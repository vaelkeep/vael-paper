Spot rates on the Asia–Europe route fell for a third consecutive week, and are
now within a fifth of where they stood before the diversions began.

The explanation offered is not that the route has reopened but that the longer
route has been absorbed: schedules have been rewritten around it, charter
capacity has been found, and the additional sailing time is now priced in rather
than surcharged. Cost has become structure.

Whether that is good news depends on the timescale you care about. The
[carriers' own guidance](https://www.reuters.com/business/) is that the higher
cost base persists whether or not the original route returns.
