Copper closed higher for the fifth consecutive session, and the story is
unchanged from the last four: demand from grid buildout and datacentre
electrification is real and forward-committed, while the supply response
remains theoretical.

New mine capacity has a lead time measured in a decade. Announcements made this
year affect the market in the middle of the next. In the interval, inventory
does the balancing, and inventory is thin.

Scrap has taken up some of the slack, as it always does at these levels, but
scrap is a stock and not a flow. It buys quarters, not years.
