Friday night is wet and Saturday is not, which settles the question the
weekend has been asking all week.

- **Great Falls.** Dry by early afternoon, the river high and loud after the
  rain. The Billy Goat Trail, section A, is the one Nell will want and the one
  the note on the previous page has a question about; the park's page says it
  reopened in July. Four hours including the drive, and the Old Angler's Inn
  serves until three. Walking shoes, not sandals — the correspondence page has
  already said so.
- **The museum**, if the forecast is wrong. The textiles rooms at the Renwick
  reopened in August and neither of you has seen them, and it is free, and it
  is dry.
- **Dinner.** The recipe you flagged last week — the one with the fennel —
  needs the fennel bought on Saturday morning, because the good stall at
  Eastern Market is Saturday only. Everything else is in the cupboard, except
  the wine. The dining page has the alternative if nobody wants to cook.

Union Station at eleven, Red Line, unaffected. Coming back, see the local
page: allow twenty-five minutes, or take the streetcar.
