The maritime corridor closed since late June reopened on Wednesday with a
convoy of four bulk carriers, escorted for the first thirty miles and then, by
agreement, not.

What has not reopened is the insurance market that made the corridor viable.
War-risk premiums remain where they were during the closure, and the report
notes that the four ships sailed under charters written before it — that is,
somebody else's risk. Whether a fifth ship sails is the actual test, and the
report does not expect an answer before the weekend.

Wheat futures fell on the news and recovered most of it by the close.
