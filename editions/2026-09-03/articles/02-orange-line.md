Metro is single-tracking the Orange Line between Stadium–Armory and Eastern
Market from this morning through the seventeenth for track and platform work,
with trains through the section every fifteen minutes instead of every six. The
Blue and Silver lines are affected the same way over the same stretch. A
shuttle bus runs between the two stations for anyone who would rather not wait.

For you the practical answer is to walk. The dentist, the library and the
market are all within twenty minutes of the house on foot, and East Capitol is
shaded most of the way. The paper has assumed that route everywhere else in
this edition.

The exception is Saturday. Nell's train comes into Union Station, which is on
the Red Line and unaffected — but the fastest way back to the Hill from there
is the Orange Line from Union Station to Eastern Market, which is exactly the
section that is crawling. Allow twenty-five minutes, or take the streetcar to
H and Eighth and walk from there, which is longer but never waits.

The Hill Rag's report adds that the platform work at Eastern Market includes
the long-promised second elevator. That is the first good news about that
station in some years.
