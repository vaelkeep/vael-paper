The season opens at home on Sunday at one, and for the first time in three
Septembers the offensive line that finishes the preseason is the one that
started it. The Ledger's preview makes that the whole story, reasonably: the
quarterback was sacked eleven fewer times in the second half of last year than
the first, and the difference was almost entirely who was standing in front of
him.

The worry has moved to the other side of the ball. The secondary lost two
starters in the spring and replaced them with a draft pick and a veteran on a
one-year deal, and the preseason offered no evidence either way, because
preseason never does. Thursday's injury report lists one cornerback as limited.
Read that as "will play, cautiously."

Practically: the game is at one, the walk to the bar on Eighth Street is
twelve minutes, and the storms forecast for Thursday will have cleared by
Sunday. Nell is a Giants fan, which is a fact to plan around rather than
mention.
