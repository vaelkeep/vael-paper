**The landlord**, about the furnace service before the season turns. He is
proposing Tuesday the eighth between nine and one, and asking whether someone
will be in. You will not; the Henley phase-two meeting is that morning. Suggest
the Wednesday, or offer to leave a key with the neighbour as you did in March.
This is the one that has been waiting since Monday.

**The library**, confirming that the book you reserved has arrived at the
Southeast branch and will be held until the ninth. No reply strictly needed,
but it is two blocks from the dentist, and today you are walking.

**Nell**, asking whether she should bring walking shoes. Yes. The weekend
pages explain why, and the short version is that Saturday looks dry and the
Billy Goat Trail will not be.
