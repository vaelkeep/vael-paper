Wednesday's loss was the third in a row and the tenth in fourteen, which is the
kind of stretch September produces when a team has two starters on the injured
list and nothing left to play for but the draft order. Tonight is a bullpen
game, which is what the manager calls it when there is no starter.

The one reason to watch is the rookie shortstop, who has reached base in
nineteen straight and is now the only thing about this month that will matter
next April.

First pitch is at 7:05 at Navy Yard. If you go, the Green Line is unaffected
by the Orange Line work. It is also, per the forecast, likely to rain by then.
