On the second of September last year you wrote a note titled *Walking*, which
begins:

> I keep planning to walk in the evenings and keep not doing it. The plan is
> the problem. The evening is when the day has already spent me. Try the
> morning, before anything has a claim on it, and try it for a fortnight before
> deciding anything.

The fortnight became the habit that the wellbeing page now reports on every
week, and you have not, as far as the notes record, walked in the evening
since. The rest of the note is a list of routes — the Anacostia loop, the
Capitol grounds, the long one out to the Arboretum — and a remark that the
Arboretum one "is only worth it before nine in September, after which it is a
walk through soup." It is September.

There is a second note from the same week, untitled, that reads in its entirety:
*Ask Nell about the Billy Goat Trail — she said section A was closed after the
storm.* It seems she never answered, or you never asked. She arrives on
Saturday.
